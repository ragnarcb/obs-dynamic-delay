ffmpeg.exe
==========

Dynamic Delay uses ffmpeg.exe only to re-encode the video of a destination that
has a bitrate of its own (for example Twitch at 6000 kbps while OBS sends 15000
to YouTube). It runs as a separate program; Dynamic Delay talks to it through
pipes.

Build:   FFmpeg 8.0.1, "essentials" build by gyan.dev
