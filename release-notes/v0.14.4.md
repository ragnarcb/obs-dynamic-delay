**A bitrate of its own never brings a platform down anymore.** If your PC cannot keep up with the re-encoding (a heavy game at 1080p60, say), Dynamic Delay restarts it, and if it keeps falling behind, that platform simply gets the stream as OBS sends it, with a note in the panel.
